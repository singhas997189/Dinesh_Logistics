About Dinesh Logistics

Dinesh Logistics is a leading logistics company based in Kolkata, specializing in container transportation, import, and export services. With years of experience in the industry, we have built a reputation for reliability, efficiency, and customer satisfaction.

Our state-of-the-art facilities and experienced team ensure that your cargo reaches its destination safely and on time. We handle everything from documentation to final delivery, making international trade seamless for our clients.

We pride ourselves on our commitment to excellence and our ability to provide customized solutions for businesses of all sizes. Our vision is to become the most trusted logistics partner in the region.
